FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

What is ConeQt?

ConeQt is a mobile app that connects people who need a service with trusted workers and service providers nearby. You can search, book and chat in one place.

When does the app launch?

Very soon (قريباً). Join the waitlist and we'll let you know as soon as ConeQt is available on the App Store and Google Play.

How much does it cost to join as a worker?

Workers join with a low-cost monthly subscription, priced to be affordable for independent workers and small businesses. Add your skills, set your availability and start getting booking requests. We'll share the exact price at launch.

What kinds of services can I find?

36 categories, including AC & HVAC, electrical, construction, home appliances, IT & tech, automotive, beauty, tutoring, childcare, photography, pet services, travel, and more.

How do I know a worker is trustworthy?

Workers on ConeQt have verified profiles. You can also chat with them before booking to make sure they're the right fit for your job.
